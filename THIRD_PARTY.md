# Third-party and provenance boundary

## Not included

The following are acknowledged as external references or Skills, but are not bundled, republished, or presented as our work:

- `Zeejay0/gathered-scenes-zine-skill` — external Zine Skills `scenes-gathered-zine-v1-3` and `scene-distillation-zine-v1-3`.
- `popopo-99/zy-cinematic-realism` — external cinematic prompt Skill.

The project also has a separate internal archive for distilled adaptations. Those files are intentionally outside this repository until upstream attribution, license compatibility, and redistribution scope are individually cleared.

## Our examples

The gallery contains 14 primary examples and one Y2K variant. Seven finished Skill outputs were supplied by the maintainer and are preserved unchanged. The remaining eight primary examples are existing project-generated or maintainer-approved assets. Individual provenance is recorded in `examples/manifest.json`.
