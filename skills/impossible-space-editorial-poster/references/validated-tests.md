# Validated Forward Tests

Use these tests only when evaluating or extending the Skill. They demonstrate decision boundaries, not layouts to reproduce.

## Motel source — demonstrated rejection boundaries

Source evidence retained: one traveler in cream clothing and boots, pale vintage convertible, weathered motel sign, desert mountains, hard noon light.

Rejected decision 1: transform the convertible into a three-bay motel cutaway. Although construction and light were coherent, the car stopped being desirable as a car, the fashion subject became a scale prop, and the dollhouse spectacle replaced the source's desert-road attitude.

Rejected decision 2: raise the road into a monumental vertical ramp. Although the car remained intact, the literal travel metaphor dominated the frame, reduced the woman and car to supporting props, and felt like a theme-park effect rather than directed editorial space.

Rejected decision 3: place a polished `LAST CHECKOUT` headline in open sky and confine the noon-versus-midnight idea to the windshield. The result improved identity, cleanliness, and color, but still read as a fashion photograph plus title because the type, car, person, and temporal effect did not share one structural action. The local windshield effect was too small to carry the poster at thumbnail size.

Rejected precursor: place a road plane beside the original foreground with one straight vertical seam. It reads as two images overlaid, lacks fold thickness and shared shadow, preserves too much of the source composition, and relies on a large title for novelty.

Required next behavior: preserve the woman, convertible, motel sign, desert-fashion attitude, and hard noon light as the first read. Test the source word `MOTEL` as a graphic seed: its letterforms, counters, and weathered sign geometry may structure scale, crop, occlusion, or a contained time shift. This is a source-specific forward-test direction, not a universal layout.

## Fault Line — displaced court planes

The forward-test image is not bundled in this public release; use the decision logic, not a copied image.

Source evidence retained: two distinct women, sage and terracotta outfits, shared tennis net, cobalt court, weathered green wall, palm shadow, tropical hard sun.

Successful decision: use the net as one continuous physical threshold between unequal blue and green plane systems. Preserve both people intact and let color, court lines, shadow, and the net explain the spatial contradiction.

Watchpoint: a simple color split is not enough. The court lines, ground contact, net continuity, shadow behavior, and subject placement must establish separate spatial systems.

## Forward-test rule

For each future route test, record only:

- source-specific evidence;
- selected spatial route;
- accepted visual act;
- one demonstrated failure boundary;
- observable identity, construction, text, and finish checks.

Do not accumulate style adjectives or treat one accepted image as a universal template.
