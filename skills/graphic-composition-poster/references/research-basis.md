# Research Basis and Originality Boundary

This Skill was developed from broad public study of contemporary poster and editorial graphic design, including varied work surfaced through Pinterest research. The reference pool is a study library, not a style source and not a layout catalogue.

Transferable methods:

- make photography one component of a larger two-dimensional system;
- establish a single visual rule before adding detail;
- let crop, type, color, and negative space share a measurable relationship;
- use contrast in scale and information density to control the first read;
- keep a clean finish when clean planes are part of the visual decision;
- treat recognisable subject matter as evidence, rather than filling the page with effects.

Non-transferable material:

- a named artist's or studio's visual identity;
- a reference's title lockup, exact word, crop, palette, grid, photo pose, signature object, or punchline;
- real logos, campaign claims, credits, dates, brands, and awards not supplied by the user.

Original work is produced by selecting a grammar from the supplied source, testing it against the swap test, and rebuilding when the composition could fit any image.
