# FLOA Mariposa brand copy contract

## Exact product name

The product name is exactly:

```text
FLOA Mariposa
```

Preserve the capitalization and the space. Do not translate it, add punctuation, or rewrite it as a generic “FLOA” or “Mariposa” label unless the user explicitly supplies an approved lockup.

## Lockup decisions

The name may be:

- a dominant headline when the product identity is the main subject;
- a compact wordmark-like lockup in a quiet margin;
- integrated into a beam, architectural edge, poster field, or typographic block;
- omitted when the user asks for a wordless visual study or when the supplied product artwork already contains the approved name.

Choose placement from the composition. Never split the name into two decorative corner labels by default.

## Copy hierarchy

When the user supplies copy, preserve it exactly and classify it as:

1. product name or approved lockup;
2. headline;
3. short explanatory line;
4. optional factual metadata.

Use as little copy as the idea allows. A short headline is better than invented editorial filler.

## Forbidden invention

Do not fabricate:

- product benefits, promises, prices, dates, awards, locations, customers, partners, or credentials;
- technical specifications or campaign codes;
- fake catalogue entries, credits, case numbers, timestamps, or “official” labels;
- a logo that was not supplied or explicitly requested as an original exploration.

If a social caption needs context, write it outside the artwork rather than baking unsupported claims into the image.

## Exact-text QA

Before delivery, compare every visible occurrence of `FLOA Mariposa` and every user-supplied phrase against the source brief. If an image model is likely to distort the text, render the text in a local layout, SVG, or compositing pass.
