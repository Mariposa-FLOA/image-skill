# FLOA Mariposa series system

Use this reference when the user asks for multiple posters, a campaign set, a carousel, or a visual identity family.

## Keep fixed

Keep these stable across a series unless the user explicitly changes them:

- the exact product name `FLOA Mariposa`;
- the confidence of scale and the editorial hierarchy;
- the rule that one visual event leads each piece;
- deliberate colour roles rather than arbitrary colour matching;
- source protection and exact-copy discipline;
- a clean, authored finish with material used purposefully.

## Allow to change

Change these from poster to poster so the series does not become a template:

- the source-derived metaphor;
- the dominant colour field and accent relationship;
- the crop, axis, or perspective;
- the position and behaviour of the name lockup;
- the degree of silhouette, geometry, photography, or collage;
- the material finish when it follows the concept;
- the amount and rhythm of supporting copy.

## Three useful series branches

### Signal

Use dark or saturated fields, luminous accents, blur, scanline, or a strong communication object. Keep the image event legible and avoid generic cyberpunk decoration.

### Threshold

Use paper or calm neutral fields, a beam, doorway, window, stair, or architectural cutout. Let the quiet zone carry anticipation.

### Orbit

Use a path, circular relation, repeated frame, film strip, or receding plane to express movement, flow, or becoming. Keep the motion readable with one dominant direction.

These are branches, not mandatory templates. Select one per piece and derive its exact geometry from the uploaded source.

## Series acceptance test

A successful series should pass both tests:

- Seen together, the pieces clearly belong to FLOA Mariposa.
- Seen separately, no two pieces look like the same layout with a colour swap.
