# Visual sentence selection

Use this reference only to choose a source-rooted metaphor. It supplies relationship patterns, not layouts to copy.

The current strongest families are **continuation** and **absence**. Select one. Continuation carries a visible source fact forward until it becomes a field; absence makes a measured void become the subject. Both require a clear anchor-to-structure handoff.

| Source evidence | Tension | Clean transformation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A cast shadow or silhouette | departure, reversal, unseen pull | The shadow continues into a directional field or path that contradicts the subject's movement. |
| Road lane, railing, cable, shoreline, or garment seam | separation, attachment, passage | The line becomes the only boundary that partitions the entire poster. |
| Wave, cloud edge, folded fabric, hair, or tree canopy | drift, overflow, uncertainty | The organic edge crosses into a flat field, making one area appear to overtake another. |
| Window, doorway, screen, reflective surface | access, confinement, attention | The opening becomes a measured absence; no extra prop or dream scene is needed. |
| Repeated poles, chairs, windows, cars, or figures | repetition, isolation, rhythm | One repeated unit is removed, enlarged, or breaks the sequence, turning a pattern into the narrative. |
| A horizon or architectural plane | waiting, distance, imbalance | The horizon tilts, shifts, or is continued as type to expose the pressure in an otherwise ordinary scene. |

## Family selection

- Choose **continuation** for shadows, roads, rails, seams, shorelines, hair, waves, or other evidence with a strong direction.
- Choose **absence** for doors, windows, wall edges, repeated grids, screens, or patterned spaces where a missing unit can be felt.
- If neither family is grounded in the source, use `graphic-composition-poster` instead of inventing a symbol.

Absence does not mean “draw a row of doors.” Use the actual rhythm of the chosen subject—windows, seats, poles, signs, shelves, faces, or building bays—and let one omission create the tension. A repeated structure is useful only when the repetition itself carries meaning.

## Pick by force, not decoration

Choose the relationship that makes a source-specific fact newly meaningful. A street photo with a strong road line wants a boundary or passage; it does not need a fabricated red thread. A portrait with no meaningful geometric evidence may be better handled by `graphic-composition-poster`.

## Test at thumbnail size

The viewer should first see the transformation, then the anchor, then the title. If they first see the title or a generic colour block, simplify or change the visual sentence.
